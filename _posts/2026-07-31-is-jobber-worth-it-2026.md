<h3>The Add-On Math Most Small Businesses Discover Only After Signing Up</h3>
<p><strong>The published prices are honest, but the real total cost of ownership often runs $400–$700/month</strong> once you add CompanyCam for photo documentation, ResponsiBid for self-quoting, and the AI Receptionist add-on. <strong>That is roughly $7,860/year</strong> for what most contractors would describe as "the basic feature set" in 2026.</p>

<p>The critical distinction: <strong>many of those add-ons aren't optional</strong> for the way field service businesses actually operate. A landscaping company that doesn't document job photos is vulnerable to client disputes. A cleaning service that can't send automated follow-ups is leaving repeat bookings on the table. A plumbing company that misses calls because nobody's in the office is losing jobs to competitors who answer.</p>

<p>None of those tools are built into Jobber's base plans. They require either third-party integrations with separate subscriptions or Jobber's own add-ons at additional cost.</p>

<p>This isn't a dishonest pricing structure — it's a modular one. The question is whether that modularity works for your budget model or against it.</p>

<p><strong>Worth it — specifically on the Connect Teams plan at $169/month for five users.</strong> This is Jobber's sweet spot. <p><strong>Rating:</strong> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐</p>

<p><strong>Worth it with an asterisk.</strong> The Grow Teams plan at $299–$349/month (10 users) unlocks the automation features that growing businesses need. Before committing, run the full add-on cost calculation for your specific feature requirements. If CompanyCam, Marketing Suite, and AI Receptionist are all on your list, the real monthly cost may be closer to $600/month — at which point the flat-rate alternatives deserve a serious evaluation.</p>
<p><strong>Rating:</strong> ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆</p>

<h3>Team of 11–15+ Technicians</h3>
<p><strong>Worth it only if the specific features you're using daily are Jobber's genuine strengths</strong> — route optimization, Client Hub, mobile app quality. <p><strong>Rating:</strong> ⭐⭐⭐☆☆</p>

<h3>Operations Needing Advanced Customization or Deep Reporting</h3>
<p><strong>Not worth it as a long-term platform.</strong> Jobber's intentional simplicity is an advantage for the businesses it's designed for and a constraint for businesses that have grown past it. Evaluate FieldPulse for customization needs or ServiceTitan for enterprise reporting depth.</p>
<p><strong>Rating:</strong> ⭐⭐☆☆☆</p>

<h2 id="who-should-avoid">🚫 Who Should Avoid Jobber?</h2>
<p>Jobber may not be the best choice if:</p>
<ul>
    <li><strong>You manage 20+ technicians.</strong> Jobber's per-user pricing and reporting limitations become significant at this scale.</li>
