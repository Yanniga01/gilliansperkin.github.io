<h3>2. Payment Processing: The Cost Nobody Puts in the Budget</h3>
<p>Card processing starts at <strong>2.59%</strong>. ACH bank transfers run <strong>1%</strong>.</p>

<div style="background: #fffbeb; border: 1px solid #fde68a; padding: 1.5rem; border-radius: 10px; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
    <p style="margin: 0; color: #92400e;"><strong>⚠️ The processing reality:</strong> A five-technician business collecting $60,000/month in payments through Housecall Pro pays approximately <strong>$1,554/month in processing fees alone — $18,648/year</strong>. That figure is often larger than the annual software subscription cost and almost never appears in pricing comparisons.</p>
</div>

<p><strong>The ACH route reduces this substantially:</strong> 1% on $60,000 is $600/month versus $1,554 for card. For businesses whose clients are willing to pay via bank transfer — common in commercial service work and higher-ticket residential jobs — steering payments toward ACH saves thousands annually.</p>

<p><strong>Housecall Pro's 2.59% rate is genuinely competitive.</strong> Jobber charges 2.9% at standard rates.
